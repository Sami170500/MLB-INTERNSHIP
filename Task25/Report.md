# Comparison Report

We compared Bicubic interpolation and Real-ESRGAN using three degraded images with known original versions.

From the visual comparison, Real-ESRGAN produced sharper and more detailed images than Bicubic. However, the PSNR and SSIM results were higher for Bicubic on all three images.

This shows that visual quality and numerical metrics do not always give the same result. Real-ESRGAN can create sharper or reconstructed details that may look better but are not exactly the same as the original pixels.

Overall, Bicubic performed better according to PSNR and SSIM, while Real-ESRGAN looked better visually.
